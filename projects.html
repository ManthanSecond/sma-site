Content-Based Video Retrieval

Analyses a video with OpenCV, flags near-duplicate frames and retrieves visually similar frames using colour histograms and ORB features.

Samples frames at 5 fps, computes brightness, sharpness, motion, dHash and HSV histograms, then ranks matches by cosine similarity and verifies them with ORB feature matching.
